=== qj2f-0943dbc686b8
[user] What is the NEXUS machine learning model?
[assistant] The only model in NEXUS today is microsoft/deberta-v3-base, fine-tuned in Phase 10 as a 14-class intent classifier. It reads one sentence and predicts which NEXUS capability the request belongs to, with a confidence. It cannot write answers, hold a conversation or explain anything, because it is a classifier and not a language model.
[user] Interesting. is the classifier reliable on real user phrasing?
[assistant] It scored about 0.974 accuracy on a held-out synthetic test set, but only 42 of 56 (75%) on hand-written natural-language requests, so real-world accuracy is noticeably lower.

=== qj2f-8eb29ee39f4b
[user] Which speech recognition does NEXUS use?
[assistant] Speech-to-text is the browser's own SpeechRecognition (webkit-prefixed in Safari). NEXUS does not ship or run a speech model, and does not use Whisper. In Chrome and Edge the audio is sent to the browser vendor's servers for transcription, and Firefox has no speech recognition at all, so typing is always available as a fallback.
[user] Understood. Now, does my voice stay on my device in NEXUS?
[assistant] Not necessarily. With Chrome or Edge your audio is transcribed on Google's or Microsoft's servers, and NEXUS's own backend only receives the resulting text.
